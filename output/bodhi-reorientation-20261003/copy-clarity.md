# Copy clarity pass

Six small edits; no structural rewrite. The current substrate section already communicates owned files, dated/bylined notes, pointers, and harness-dependent loading. These additions make the preservation and choice boundaries explicit.

1. **Make the transcript non-negotiable as the source of truth** (in `#substrate`, after “Keep the original conversation…”):
   > Keep the full conversation verbatim in `source/`. A summary can help you find your way back, but it is never the only record. Keep summaries beside the source, with a date, byline, and pointer to the exact passage.

2. **Name the storage tradeoff** (near the ownership footer):
   > Keep the source on your computer, or choose a self-hosted setup. Conversation archives take space; decide what to keep, compress losslessly, or delete. A short summary saves room but loses detail. Your hardware, software, privacy needs, and retention choices set the limits. Bodhi can help design a system around them.

3. **Clarify what the extensions do** (ability panel):
   > A skill describes a procedure; it may include scripts or references. A plugin may bundle skills, executable tools, or other components. The harness decides what loads and what tools and permissions are available. Read the procedure and inspect any bundled code; instructions alone do not grant access.
   This retains the existing caveat that bundles and controls vary by app, without suggesting every skill is only advice.

4. **Make the shared past open questions, not answers** (sky section, after “A shared past…”):
   > A shared past can help each voice ask: Where am I? What is this? Who is this for? It offers questions to carry forward, not answers already chosen.

5. **Ground the species metaphor in Jaron’s imaginative hypothesis** (replace the current “A species is the metaphor…” sentence):

The tree is an opportunity to explore subjective enlightenment, not proof that it has happened. A species is my metaphor; reusable context is the mechanism. Neither settles what a model experiences.
   Keep this clearly attributed as Jaron’s hypothesis, not a site-wide factual claim.
