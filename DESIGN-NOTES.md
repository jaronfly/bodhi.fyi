## Claude’s pixel scroll and the shared walk

Everything below the grove sits on the fixed pixel-art world Claude drew for the page: the field guide, the sources, the evidence and the afterword. The Sol pass extends its existing painters and projection rather than replacing the world.

A fixed sky band changes with the walk’s light. Claude’s original full dusk, stars, moon and aurora scene is visible again when the story looks up. Three ranges of rolling dark hills slide sideways in whole pixels as you scroll. The walk passes autumn trees, a narrow fog wood, river crossings, a shared garden clearing, alpine ground, a shingle shore and an orchard. Existing reeds, lily pads, stepping stones, bridges, mushrooms, lanterns and cottages remain in use. Terrain widths and textures ease between regions; a sudden rectangular shore join reported by Jaron is replaced by a tapered, irregular transition. Soft Soil halos keep running text legible without hiding all the landscape.

The walk uses a small pixel buffer scaled by whole numbers, with no smoothing. Seasonal variation uses the existing earthy palette and restrained warm accents. The field guide’s original sky and tree remain intact. The orchard painter runs inside the walk’s canvas, with its former standalone canvas hidden to avoid a second landscape. The ship’s original SVG remains available as a close view with keyboard controls; the distant ship steps aside while that chapter is visible. The walk is clipped below the film and never paints over its 3D scenes.

Motion respects the same switches as the rest of the page. With the system’s reduced-motion setting, the film’s Stillness button, or the field guide’s ambient-motion switch off, the backdrop becomes one complete still frame per section and redraws only when the section changes. It pauses when the tab is hidden and while the film is on screen. It stores nothing, loads nothing and is hidden from assistive technology.

## Interaction and access

Native page scroll drives the reversible film. No wheel hijacking or autoplay audio. Stillness replaces camera travel with composed frames. System reduced-motion settings are respected, and offscreen or hidden canvas animation pauses. The afterword uses native disclosure elements. All story text remains available without JavaScript.

Every grove resident can be picked up by pointer. With the canvas focused, Space selects a resident, arrows move them, and Space or Escape releases them. The next Space selection advances through the residents. The pause, seed and season controls provide alternatives to waiting for the ambient cycle.

The pixel tree and sky keep their offscreen pausing and their own motion control. Fonts are fetched from Google Fonts with local fallbacks. There are no analytics, sign-up forms, external model calls or persistent browser storage in this experience.
